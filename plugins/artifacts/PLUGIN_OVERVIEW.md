The Claude app's Artifacts page, inside BB: your claude.ai artifacts and the pages your BB chats save, in one gallery.

## What you get

- An **Artifacts** page in the left rail, with previews, tabs and search.
- **Share** publishes a BB page to your claude.ai artifacts, copies the link and opens it in the Claude app, where Claude's Share menu sets who can open it.
- **Install as app** opens the artifact in the Claude desktop app, ready for Claude's own Install as app: a real Mac or Windows app with its own window, Claude and your connectors.
- A `bb artifacts` command that agents can use to publish and list artifacts.

## How it works

It uses your own Claude Code, signed in to claude.ai, with only its Artifact tool, which is the documented way to list and publish artifacts. Pages come from each chat's `artifacts` folder and any extra folders you add. Publishing never edits your files, and an update never overwrites changes made on claude.ai.

## Needs

BB 0.49+, Claude Code 2.1.281+ signed in to claude.ai (Pro, Max, Ultra, Team or Enterprise), the Claude desktop app 6.28899+ for Install as app, and Google Chrome for previews.
